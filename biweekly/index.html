Vesta Supply & Stockouts

Croissant supply — SZ: Vesta formally notified us on Sep 30 that it is at full capacity and cannot produce new products. SZ croissants can therefore only be bridged by an outside supplier — Baxun Food (a BreadTalk South-China factory, croissant fat content adjustable).
This is a short-term emergency fix only — Baxun's lines already run over-saturated; the arrangement keeps the new stores opening on schedule.
Feedback on Vesta product stockout

This is by no means a complaint — we are only pointing out the current issues and difficulties. We fully understand that Sam's orders are extremely important and must be prioritized above all other tasks.

The SH store still has a clear dependence on Vesta. If Vesta runs out of stock, our sales will definitely be affected — that is an undeniable fact. Moon Cake

  • Sold 18,000 boxes this year — budget met, broadly flat year-on-year. In the current market climate, holding volume steady is already a commendable achievement.
  • The mooncake market kept contracting in 2026 — overall size down about −10% YoY — on two drivers: persistent contraction in corporate bulk-purchase & business-gifting demand (clients' procurement budgets cut), and consumer demand for pure mooncake products continuing to decline.
  • Besides consolidating existing clients' orders, we brought supplier VESTA into procurement cooperation — 2,550 boxes, crucial support for hitting the overall budget target.
